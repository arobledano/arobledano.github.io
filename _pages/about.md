Welcome everyone!

I'm Alvaro, a postdoctoral researcher at the Instituto Pirenaico de Ecología [CroyPyr Group (IPE-CSIC)](https://cryopyr.csic.es/) in Zaragoza (Spain). My research is mainly focused on modelling how snow and radiation interact at different scales, spanning from sunlight propagation at micrometric scales (optical properties of snow), to snowpack energy balance modelling in mountainous terrain. I'm now starting to delve deeper in the use of data assimilation techniques to improve snowpack modelling at IPE-CSIC. 

This website is still work in progress and will progressively be updated. If you're interested in reaching out, feel free to contact me through any of the options on the sidebar.

Research topics
======

- Snow optical properties
- Topography controls on the snowpack energy balance
- Snow hydrology
- Data assimilation
